How the economy works

Harvesters mine ore (gold) and gems (blue, worth about twice as much), then unload at a Refinery. Mined ore needs storage: each Refinery holds 2,000 and each Silo 1,500. When storage is full, harvesters wait and you hear “Silos needed”. Ore fields slowly regrow; gems do not. Building costs are paid gradually while construction runs, so running out of credits pauses production rather than refusing it.

Naval warfare: build a Naval Yard on water within six tiles of your base (Twin Seas maps put a harbor right next to you). Submarines are invisible while submerged; only sonar (Patrol Boats, Destroyers, Missile Boats and the Naval Yard) or a sub firing its weapons reveals them, and only depth charges can hit a submerged sub. Ships can shell the shore and tanks and turrets on the coast can shoot back.

Air force: an Airfield parks four jets. Select a jet and right click an enemy for an attack run, the ground to patrol, or the airfield to land. Jets fly back on their own to rearm and repair when they run out of ammo. Stealth jets are invisible until they get close to the enemy or fire. Parked fighters scramble by themselves against enemy aircraft and missiles. The SR-71 Blackbird flies a recon pass wherever you click and reveals everything along its path.

Patrols (tanks, infantry, ships, helicopters and jets): select units and press the Patrol button at the bottom of the sidebar (or the patrol key, default O), then click a spot. The units patrol back and forth between where they are and that spot, attacking anything they meet and then returning to the route. To make a longer loop, hold Shift while clicking several waypoints and right click to finish. Jets on patrol fly home to rearm when they run out of ammo and then return to their route by themselves. Spy satellites: build a Satellite Center (needs a Tech Center) and launch satellites from the sidebar button (default key K). Right click to park a satellite anywhere; it watches a wide area and reveals stealth jets. Fog of war: areas stay visible for about 14 seconds after your units leave before fading back.

Supply depots: Land, Air and Sea Supply Depots (Base tab) can be built anywhere you have explored except right next to the enemy. They stockpile supplies over time, and the farther they are from your base the faster they produce (up to 2.5×). Supply Trucks drive them to your Refinery or Construction Yard, Cargo Planes fly them to your Airfield, and Supply Ships sail them to your Naval Yard; each delivery is paid in cash. Each depot comes with one free transport. The convoys are unarmed, so guard the route.

Anti-air: Flak Turrets and SAM Sites (Defense tab) and Flak Tanks and SAM Tanks (Vehicles tab) automatically shoot at aircraft, and they always go after incoming missiles first. Ballistic and strategic missiles are big and slow, so a few SAM Sites around your base can knock them down before they land.

Power plants feed everything else. If demand passes output you get low power: production runs at half speed, the radar goes dark, turrets fire slower, and Arc Towers shut off. Damaged power plants produce less.
